UWC Commute Club

Fuel Lucky Draw

Rules for the second-semester R1,000 fuel-voucher draw.

1-31 August 2026

Draw rules

Prize
One fuel voucher worth R1,000. The prize is not exchangeable for cash.
Who may enter
All current University of the Western Cape students with a valid 7-digit student number may enter.
Promotion period
The draw opens on 1 August 2026 and closes at 23:59 SAST on 31 August 2026.
Valid entry
An eligible person must have at least one active UWC Commute Club pool interest at the closing time. A person who joined before the opening date is also eligible if at least one of their pool interests remains active at closing. There is no entry fee.
Number of entries
Each eligible person receives one entry, regardless of how many pools they join. Duplicate or invalid entries may be excluded.
No contact-sharing requirement
Entrants do not have to form a carpool, accept a lift, consent to contact-detail sharing, or communicate with other pool members to qualify.
Winner selection
One winner will be selected at random from all eligible unique entrants on 1 September 2026.
Contacting the winner
The winner will be contacted through the official UWC contact details associated with the student number submitted to the service. Eligibility may be verified before the prize is awarded.
Response period
If the selected winner cannot be verified or does not respond within five working days, another eligible entrant may be selected at random.
Privacy
Student numbers will be used to identify unique eligible entrants and administer the draw. Participation in the draw does not change the UWC Commute Club's contact-sharing consent requirements. A winner's identity will not be published without permission.
Administration
The prize is funded and administered by the University of the Western Cape.

The University may amend, postpone, or cancel the draw where reasonably necessary. Any material change will be communicated on this page.
