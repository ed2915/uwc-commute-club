Direction

Privacy and consent

UWC Commute Club collects your student or staff number, direction, suburb, and selected travel time to record pool interest.

For students, an email address can be derived from the student number and will not be shared unless the student gives explicit consent later.

A staff number cannot be used to derive an email address. Staff will therefore be asked for their UWC email address in a separate window and must consent to its storage and sharing when needed to facilitate a pool connection.

Public pool tables show aggregate counts only. Admin access is token-protected.

Removing a pool deletes it from the active database. Encrypted disaster-recovery backups and Render snapshots may retain it temporarily under their limited retention periods. Recovery copies are used only after data loss, not for matching or contact sharing.

Have at least one active pool on 31 August 2026 to receive one entry in the R1,000 fuel-voucher draw. View the draw rules.

If other people are already in this pool, the organiser can follow up with a consent email before any contact details are shared.

Enter your UWC email address

A six-digit staff number does not allow UWC Commute Club to generate your email address automatically.

Your UWC email address contains your name and is personal information. If you submit it, you consent to UWC Commute Club storing it and sharing it when necessary to facilitate contact with people in a relevant pool.

Your address will not be shown in the public pool tables.
