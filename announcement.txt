UWC Commute Club

As the second semester begins, students and staff are invited to use the UWC
Commute Club to find others travelling to or from campus from similar areas and
at similar times.

The service allows UWC commuters to indicate their suburb, travel direction and
usual travel times. It then shows where commute pools are forming, using
aggregate counts only. Individual details are not shown publicly, and contact
details are not shared automatically.

By making shared commute interests more visible, the UWC Commute Club can help
students and staff explore possible lift clubs, reduce fuel costs, and
contribute to a greener, quieter campus with fewer cars on the road.

Join at least one commute pool and stand a chance to win a R1,000 fuel voucher.
All current UWC students and staff with at least one active pool on 31 August
2026 will be entered. One entry per person. The draw closes at 23:59 SAST on
31 August 2026, and the winner will be selected at random on 1 September 2026.
Joining the draw does not require consenting to contact-detail sharing.

Full draw rules:
https://uwc-commute-club.onrender.com/draw-rules.html

Users can check which pools they have joined and remove themselves at any time.
